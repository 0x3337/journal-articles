# What's new in Axia 1.7

In Axia 1.7 we dressed the interface in Liquid Glass, taught it to track debts, rebuilt Settings, added filters to Activity, and made a lot of other improvements.

![Axia 1.7: Liquid Glass, Debts, and more](media/axia-1-7.jpg)

## Fresh and clear

We redrew every screen and every icon. You'll notice it most in the small things: rounder corners, a calmer palette, and an activity preview that softly blurs the screen behind it. We don't think Axia has ever looked this light.

![Axia screenshots](media/screenshots.jpg)

Settings didn't just get a new look — it was rebuilt from the ground up. Navigating it is simpler now, and there's more to do there.

```tabs
Old | media/settings-old.jpg
*New | media/settings-new.jpg
```

First stop: backup — export all your data to JSON and load it back whenever you need it. The same screen is where you choose how Axia groups your totals — by day, week, month, or year. And the theme can now be set independently of the system one: keep Axia light even when the whole phone is dark.

At the very bottom of Settings we gathered links to our social accounts — follow us to be the first to hear what's new in Axia.

## Who owes whom

Lent a friend some cash and forgot all about it? Axia remembers for you now. Create a debt, choose I owe or Owe me, then set the amount, the account, and the person.

![New debt screen: the I owe / Owe me switch, an amount field, a contact and an account](media/debt.jpg)

Add a person right in the app or pull one from Contacts. The I owe and Owe me cards on the main screen show the total and the balance for each one. If you've lent in several currencies, Axia shows each of them separately and sums everything up in your main one.

## Filters and periods in Activity

Filter your activity by account, category, and currency. Tap an account on the main screen or a category in Insights, and Axia opens Activity with the right filter already applied.

![Activity screen with a filter bar: period, Accounts, Category, Currency](media/activity-filters.jpg)

We rebuilt the period picker from scratch: a Day / Week / Month / Year switch with the periods themselves right below it. Picking the one you need or jumping to the next takes a single tap.

![Period picker: the year 2026, Month mode and a grid of twelve months with June selected](media/period-picker.jpg)

## And more

- **Category icons.** They're vector-based now and stay crisp on any screen. We drew 11 new ones: a bank, a barbershop, a brain, a building, a coffee bean, fireworks, a gear, a mountain, popcorn, a puzzle piece, and a wrench with a screwdriver.
- **Account order.** Drag accounts around however you like. Axia computes balances from the activities themselves, so they always agree with the list.
- **Transfers between currencies.** Axia converts the amount at the exchange rate for you.
- **Calculator.** Copy and paste amounts.
- **Insights.** The average line on the chart shows where you went beyond your usual spending.
- **Widget.** Exchange rates right on the Home Screen.
- **Apple Shortcuts.** A new Convert Currency action. In Apple Pay automations, Axia picks the category by the merchant's name on its own.
- **Five new languages.** German, French, Portuguese, Turkish, and Traditional Chinese. We translated the currency names into every language too.

We put more into 1.7 than into any update before it. We hope you enjoy it.
