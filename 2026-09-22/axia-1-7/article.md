# What's new in Axia 1.7

We've released Axia 1.7, the biggest update in the app's history. We dressed the interface in Liquid Glass, taught Axia to track debts, rebuilt "Settings" and added filters to "Activity".

![Axia 1.7: Liquid Glass, Debts, and more](media/axia-1-7.jpg)

## Fresh and translucent

We redrew the interface and the icons in the Liquid Glass style. Glass elements let the content show through softly, and the updated palette makes every screen lighter and cleaner.

![Axia Screenshots](media/screenshots.jpg)

"Settings" didn't just get a new look, they were rebuilt from the ground up. Navigating them is simpler now, and there's more to do there.

```tabs
Old | media/settings-old.jpg
*New | media/settings-new.jpg
```

Among the new things is a backup: export all your data to JSON and load it back. The same place sets the period Axia shows your totals and other data for — day, week, month, or year. And the theme can now be set independently of the system one: keep Axia light even when the whole phone is dark.

At the very bottom of "Settings" we gathered links to our social media — follow us to be the first to hear what's new in Axia.

## Who owes whom

Axia now remembers who owes you and whom you owe. Create a debt, choose "I owe" or "Owe me", then set the amount, the account, and the person.

![New debt screen: the "I owe" / "Owe me" switch, an amount field, a contact and an account](media/debt.jpg)

Add a person right in the app or pull one from Contacts. The "I owe" and "Owe me" cards on the accounts screen show the total and the balance for each one. If you lent in several currencies, Axia shows each of them separately and sums everything up in your main one.

## Filters and periods in "Activity"

Filter activities by account, category, and currency. Tap an account on the home screen or a category in analytics, and Axia opens "Activity" with the right filter already applied.

!["Activity" screen with a filter bar: period, "Accounts", "Category", "Currency"](media/activity-filters.jpg)

We rebuilt the date picker from scratch: a "Day / Week / Month / Year" switch with the periods themselves right below it. Picking the one you need or jumping to the next takes a single tap.

![Period picker: the year 2026, "Month" mode and a grid of twelve months with June selected](media/period-picker.jpg)

## And more

- **Category icons.** They're vector now and stay crisp on any screen. We drew 11 new ones: a bank, a barbershop, a brain, a building, a coffee bean, fireworks, a gear, a mountain, popcorn, a puzzle piece, and a wrench with a screwdriver.
- **Account order.** Drag accounts around however you like. Axia computes balances from transactions, so they always agree with "Activity".
- **Transfers between currencies.** Axia converts the amount at the exchange rate for you.
- **Calculator.** Copy and paste amounts.
- **Analytics.** The average line on the chart shows where you went beyond your usual spending.
- **Widget.** Exchange rates right on the Home Screen.
- **Shortcuts.** Create an entry and convert currencies with your voice. In Apple Pay automations, Axia picks the category by the merchant's name on its own.
- **Five new languages.** German, French, Portuguese, Turkish, and Traditional Chinese. We translated the currency names into every language too.
